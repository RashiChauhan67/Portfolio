About

I'm an AI/ML engineer and Technical Consultant at HPE. My work covers production ML, data engineering, automation, forecasting and applied AI systems. Alongside industry work, I do research in computer vision, deep learning and generative AI, published in IEEE, Elsevier and Springer venues.

My research focuses on lightweight attention-based networks for medical, agricultural and remote-sensing images. My applied work includes hybrid retrieval (RAG) over long reports and ML pipelines for data platforms.
