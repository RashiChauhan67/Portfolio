Hybrid RAG

esg-hybrid-rag

Question answering over long ESG PDF reports with dense + BM25 retrieval fused by Reciprocal Rank Fusion.

PythonSentence-TransformersBM25RRFOCR
Case study
Problem
Long, dense sustainability reports are hard to search. Keyword search misses paraphrases and embeddings alone miss exact terms and figures.
Approach
PDF pages are processed into page images and OCR text (PyMuPDF, pdfplumber). A dense encoder and a sparse BM25 index each retrieve candidate pages, and Reciprocal Rank Fusion merges the two ranked lists so the best pages rise to the top before answer generation.
Result
A complete, documented pipeline with an architecture diagram, runnable notebook and requirements.

FMANet

Colorectal tissue classification from H&E histopathology tiles with fused multi-level attention features.

TensorFlow / Keras 3AttentionHistopathologyTested · CI
Case study
Problem
Classify 128 × 128 histopathology tiles into tissue types (8 classes on Kather 5,000; 9 on CRC-VAL-HE-7K).
Approach
Low-level and high-level feature streams, each refined by a dilated residual-inception block, are pooled and embedded. Constraint fusion weights each stream by its cosine similarity to the combined features before a softmax classifier.
Result
95.0% test accuracy (macro F1 0.95) on Kather 5,000 and 97.35% (macro F1 0.97) on CRC-VAL-HE-7K, with 5.39 M parameters. Published in Computers and Electrical Engineering, 2025.

kidney-ct-attention-cnn

Lightweight dual-attention CNN that classifies kidney CT slices as cyst, normal, stone or tumor.

TensorFlow / Keras 3CT imagingAttentionTested · CI
Case study
Problem
Classify 12,446 kidney CT images into four classes (cyst 3,709; normal 5,077; stone 1,377; tumor 2,283) with a model small enough for real-time deployment.
Approach
Three stacked convolution blocks are concatenated for feature reuse, then refined by residual spatial-attention and residual channel-attention modules before a small MLP.
Result
99.48% overall accuracy as reported in the paper (80:20 train/test split), with about 0.22 M parameters. Per-class recall is 1.00 except stone (0.94). Published at IEEE ICCCNT 2024.

wbc-multistream-cnn

Three-stream CNN with gated feature fusion for white-blood-cell classification.

TensorFlow / Keras 3OpenCVMedical imagingTested · CI
Case study
Problem
Classify 5,000 microscope images into five white-blood-cell types despite stain variation.
Approach
Each image becomes three views: raw, optical density (a stain-independent colour space) and an anisotropic-diffusion smoothing of the optical density. Each view has its own small CNN and a sigmoid gate re-weights the concatenated features.
Result
97.90% test accuracy on 1,000 held-out images (stratified 80/20 split), 451,333 parameters.

carotid-ultrasound-segmentation

Lightweight attention network that segments the common carotid artery in ultrasound images.

TensorFlow / Keras 3SegmentationUltrasoundTested · CI
Case study
Problem
Predict a binary artery mask from 256 × 256 ultrasound images using 1,100 expert-annotated image/mask pairs.
Approach
Depthwise-separable encoder, an ASPP-like multi-scale module, attention gates on skip connections, squeeze-and-excitation, coordinate attention and a boundary-refinement branch. The pipeline is built to avoid train/test leakage between frames of the same scan.
Result
A 171,653-parameter model with a tested, leakage-free, scan-aware training pipeline (Dice + BCE loss; Dice, IoU, precision, recall, F1 and specificity metrics).

wildfire-spread-prediction

Next-day wildfire spread prediction from remote-sensing, weather and terrain data.

TensorFlow / Keras 3Remote sensingSegmentationTested · CI
Case study
Problem
Given a 64 × 64 km patch for one day, predict which pixels will be on fire the next day.
Approach
An Attention U-Net (32.5 M parameters) over a 12-channel stack of elevation, weather and vegetation inputs, trained with a focal + Dice loss and evaluated with IoU.
Result
Pixel accuracy is high from the first epoch because most pixels do not burn, so IoU is the primary metric. The repo documents the full training log and sample predictions, including misses.

aerial-vehicle-detection

YOLOv8 vehicle detection and classification in aerial drone imagery, fine-tuned on the VAID dataset.

Ultralytics YOLOv8Object detectionPythonTested · CI
Case study
Problem
Find vehicles in top-down drone images and classify them into seven types (sedan, minibus, truck, pickup, bus, cement truck, trailer).
Approach
Fine-tunes a pretrained YOLOv8m (25.8 M parameters) with thin wrappers around Ultralytics for dataset configuration, training, prediction and validation, plus a dataset check that flags classes missing from a split.
Result
Validation precision 0.942, recall 0.980, mAP50 0.995, mAP50-95 0.851, about 7.4 ms per image on a T4 (small validation set, as noted in the repo).

DARINet

Compact dual self-attention residual-inception network for plant disease identification.

TensorFlow / Keras 3AttentionAgricultureTested · CI
Case study
Problem
Classify leaf images by disease for cassava (5 classes), rice (3) and maize (4).
Approach
A modified inception block with depthwise-separable convolutions, a residual connection and two attention modules (channel and spatial), about 0.65 M parameters. The network is identical across datasets; only the output layer changes.
Result
Test accuracy 97.9% on rice, 94% on maize and 77% on cassava (the hardest of the three). Published at IEEE ICUMT 2023.
